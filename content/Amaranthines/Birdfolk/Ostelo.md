# Ostelo the Sacrifice

![[Ostelo_banner.webp]]

|**Attribute**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|**Alignment**|Neutral Good|
|**Domains**|Community, Peace, Twilight|
|**Pantheon**|Amaranthine|
|**Symbol**|A curved needle piercing a heart|

**Dogma:** _Ostelo, let my heart guide me. Pain blooms after loss only because it was planted in love, and both should be cherished._

**[[Ostelo]]** is the Amaranthine of love, loss, and sacrifice. She is most often depicted as a hummingbird with long, swooping tail feathers and a floating skull for a head. Her eye sockets bloom with flowers, and her curved beak drips blood. Though her depictions tend to be morbid, she is considered one of the gentlest Amaranthines.

Ostelo is the patron spirit of the **[[Sylph|sylphs]]**. It is said she understands what the inner self truly loves and that she sheds petal tears whenever hearts deny their true calling. She spurs **[[Sylph|sylphs’]]** wanderlust by allowing them to feel the pull of their hearts more strongly than other folk. While some simply follow Ostelo’s first teaching of following their passions, devout followers also try to embody Ostelo’s altruistic courage and kindle selflessness within themselves.

Folk are drawn to pray to Ostelo in moments of great love and loss. When one’s future hangs in the balance, it is said Ostelo hovers behind your back. Ostelo is also known as the silent one. She brings solace to those in grief and celebrates the kinds of love that words cannot express. Her clerics work tirelessly to understand their enigmatic Amaranthine, with no expectation that they will ever hear a message from her.

Ostelo’s temples are places of comfort and contemplation for those who have faced loss or are at a crossroads in life. They are adorned with flowers and skulls, real and carved, as a reminder that the cycle of life and death is natural. Those who have sacrificed themselves to save others are enshrined at Ostelo’s temples, and it is believed that Ostelo personally escorts them to **[[Tyton]]** the **[[Nightfather|Nightfather’s]]** lands of the dead.

In contrast with Ostelo’s silence, her temples are filled with music as her clerics sing the songs that their Amaranthine cannot. They are popular places to celebrate marriages, births, adoptions, celebrations of life, and any other ceremony that expresses love.
