# Khaluz the Bold

![[Khaluz_banner.webp]]

|**Attribute**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|**Alignment**|Lawful Good|
|**Domains**|Knowledge, Light, Order|
|**Pantheon**|Amaranthine|
|**Symbol**|A cat’s eye with the sun as its pupil|

**Dogma:** _May your light show us what is needed. Be brave, be true to yourself, and let the light illuminate the truth._

Sister to **[[Eresutha]]**, Khaluz is the Amaranthine of the sun, courage, and leadership. She is depicted as a sabertoothed lioness surrounded by a mane of brilliant solar fire. The siblings’ legendary duels are told in many tales, and the creation of the **[[Tanglewilds]]** is attributed to their blood and tears. Yet, the sisters share a deep love. Solar eclipses are said to occur when Khaluz and Eresutha draw close and nestle together in mutual forgiveness. During this time, folk can ask for a great boon to be granted by both Amaranthines.

In contrast to her mysterious sibling, Khaluz is forthright and clear in her expectations for her followers—be brave. Khaluz’s light illuminates the truth, and she calls folk to be true to themselves and their convictions. The **[[Eluran]]** leaders of the Tanglewilds claim to be her regents, chosen by the Amaranthine to rule until the day she descends from the sky to claim the throne from them. Khaluz is also revered as an Amaranthine who brings understanding through her illuminating light. Legends say she created the **[[Eluran Script]]** and gifted it to her folk so that they could come to know each other better.

The roar of the **[[Eluran|sun elurans]]** is said to be a blessing from Khaluz, allowing them to express their inner fire as a magical sound. Leaders seek the blessing of Khaluz to rouse their people with fiery speeches, while fighters and paladins look to her to find the strength to do what is necessary.

As the sun can be seen in the sky over all of **[[Everden]]**, Khaluz enjoys widespread worship, except in areas like **[[Humblewood]]**, where her role is ascribed to **[[Ardea]]**, the **[[Dawnmother]]**, instead. Elsewhere, Khaluz is thought to have a special relationship with Ardea, and tales often speak of them traveling together with the solar Amaranthine protecting the Dawnmother.

Khaluz’s temples allow light to pour through stained glass or paneless openings. Lines of shadow and sunlight formed by pillars or windows clearly mark paths that her followers travel to reach Khaluz’s golden altars, where offerings of meat are traditional.
