# Ranzang the Connector

![[Ranzang_banner.webp]]

| **Attribute** | **Details**                              |
| ------------- | ---------------------------------------- |
| **Alignment** | Lawful Neutral                           |
| **Domains**   | Community, Life, Nature                  |
| **Pantheon**  | Amaranthine                              |
| **Symbol**    | A paw-shaped honeycomb with raised claws |

**Dogma:** _Do as you intend. Be as you are intended. Honor your bonds, for there is no distinction between nature and civilization; all are one within the Rhythm._

**[[Ranzang]]** is the Amaranthine of connection. He is depicted as a six-legged badger with strong claws and a coat of intersecting stripes. Ranzang is said to dwell within a great underground network of tunnels that extends throughout **[[Everden]]**. Although tales are told of folk falling into these tunnels and being accidentally whisked away to distant lands on the Amaranthine’s back, most clerics say that the tunnels are metaphorical, representing the interconnectedness of all things.

Ranzang tends to the **[[Great Rhythm]]** with care, nurturing bonds the same way a skilled gardener recognizes the relationships between a seed, soil, water, air, and sunlight. He encourages all to honor the innate bond they have with the Great Rhythm, as one cannot approach it with dishonesty in one’s heart. Those who keep their word please the Amaranthine.

His devout followers push themselves to expand their circle of empathy to encompass not only those like them but stones, rivers, trees, and all living creatures. There is no distinction between nature and civilization in Ranzang’s teachings, as everyone is as much a part of the natural world as the plants and animals are.

As the patron Amaranthine of the **[[Mustel|mustels]]**, Ranzang instills the value of faithfulness into his people. **[[Mustel]]** culture reveres the sanctity of oaths as a form of unbreakable bond. Devout **[[Mustel|mustels]]** may still be relatively solitary rather than gregarious, as they believe the quality and depth of one’s connections are more important than the quantity. To be insincere is considered almost as bad as breaking an oath. Ranzang’s clerics teach that to best connect with others, one must first be on good terms with oneself and the land.

Large temples to Ranzang are built underground and used for commitment ceremonies of all kinds. When a solemn agreement is made within, **[[Oath Stone|oath stones]]** are exchanged as a sacred reminder. Smaller shrines to Ranzang often serve practical purposes, such as well-stocked lodges for weary travelers.
