# Eresutha the Hidden

![[Eresutha_banner.webp]]

|**Attribute**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|**Alignment**|Chaotic Good|
|**Domains**|Arcana, Night, Trickery|
|**Pantheon**|Amaranthine|
|**Symbol**|A cat’s eye with a crescent moon as its pupil|

**Dogma:** _Light my way and confuse my enemies. Guidance is found in the silver light, but wisdom is found in the shadows._

When **[[Ardea|Ardea’s]]** dawn and **[[Tyton|Tyton’s]]** night first encountered the sparks of creation, their primal brilliance was so great that the dawn and the night split into two. Amidst gold and silver rays, two feline Amaranthines were born. As young celestial beings they bounded, tussled, and pounced across the sky. They were the **[[Sisters of Light]]**—Eresutha, Amaranthine of the moon, and her sibling, **[[Khaluz]]**, Amaranthine of the sun. Together, they share the responsibility of being the patrons of the **[[Eluran|elurans]]**.

Eresutha casts her silver light on hidden paths as the Amaranthine of the moon, magic, and mysteries. She is depicted as a pale snow leopard with one shining silver eye—the other was scratched out by her sister during their great quarrel that created the **[[Tanglewilds]]**. The opening and closing of Eresutha’s remaining eye stewards the moon's phases. A mercurial Amaranthine, Eresutha guides her followers with her bright full moon aspect and vexes them with her dark form as the new moon.

**[[Eluran|Moon elurans]]** credit Eresutha with their magical healing abilities. It is said that after every sibling fight, Eresutha licks **[[Khaluz|Khaluz’s]]** wounds and purrs away her pain as the sisters forgive each other. Wizards, warlocks, and rogues seek Eresutha's blessing when seeking out knowledge, attempting artful deceptions, or hiding their intentions.

Eresutha has a deep but complicated connection with **[[Hath]]** the Whisperer, the equally mysterious Amaranthine of the stars. Some believe they are one being, while others say Eresutha hunts Hath during the full moon and is the reason Hath is so distant from the world. Another legend told in distant lands beyond **[[Wesden]]** holds that Eresutha wasn’t the original Amaranthine of the moon but that she claimed the position after slaying her predecessor.

Bards sing of Eresutha’s mystical labyrinth upon the moon, where she resides during the day. Her temples reflect her legendary abode as they are built to be maze-like and intentionally confusing to outsiders. The winding corridors are inlaid with silver clues, but some say her clerics can move the walls. Those few supplicants who reach the central shrine at a temple’s heart may ask her for a blessing. However, one can never be sure whether Eresutha will grant the request or bestow the asker with an unsolicited gift of strange knowledge instead.
