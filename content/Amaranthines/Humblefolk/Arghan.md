# Arghan the Unbroken

![[Arghan_banner.webp]]

|**Attribute**|**Details**|
|---|---|
|**Alignment**|Chaotic Neutral|
|**Domains**|Forge, Knowledge, Tempest|
|**Pantheon**|Amaranthine|
|**Symbol**|A pair of horns above a mountain|

**Dogma:** _My resolve is stone, my heart the sky. All deserve freedom, and freedom can be claimed by those willing to speak their truth and stand their ground._

Arghan is the Amaranthine of willpower, mountains, and the sky. He is usually depicted as a mighty mountain goat with large, curved horns and fur that fades into clouds. His horns and hooves are composed of craggy mountain stone that crackles with lightning.

In the dawning days of **[[Everden]]**, Arghan clashed with his elder brother, **[[Cairith]]**, in an argument that shook the world to its roots. Arghan could not bear the sluggish pace at which Cairith nurtured the growth of the **[[Cervan|cervans]]**. The two brothers fought, horns locked, their steaming breath forming great clouds. Just when it seemed Cairith had bested Arghan, Arghan stamped his mighty hooves with such force that the earth folded beneath him, splitting northern **[[Wesden]]** in two and forming the mighty **[[Horned Ridge Mountains]]** between **[[Humblewood]]** and **[[Firnveldt]]**. As Arghan rose high into the clouds atop the perilous peaks, he vowed never to be bridled again. The **[[Cervan|cervans]]** who followed him across these new mountains became the first **[[Capran|caprans]]**.

As the patron Amaranthine of the **[[Capran|caprans]]**, Arghan taught his folk to live where land and sky meet so they could test their mettle. Though mountains are Arghan’s domain, his clerics in the grasslands of **[[Firnveldt]]** believe that elevation does not matter, only the willingness to face challenges wherever they are found. Arghan’s followers pray for his blessings when their will is being tested, but they never ask for his aid, believing that fortune is forged through actions and deeds, not through divine intervention.

Temples to Arghan stand open to the sky, and ceremonies honoring him occur no matter the weather. Piles of stones called cairns are constructed by his followers in wild places to mark long journeys and triumphs over adversity. At the Great Gathering in **[[Firnveldt]]**, communities hold a **[[Grand Clash]]** in Arghan’s honor. These competitions are a mix of heated philosophical debates, tests of endurance, and outright brawls.
