# Way of the Wrangler Monk

Across the sweeping grasslands of **[[Firnveldt]]** and beyond, the monks who follow the Way of the Wrangler guide herds of animals safely across the wilds. A wrangler is never found without their trusty whip, which they use to maneuver all manner of creatures around the battlefield.

Wranglers are often seen as reckless daredevils, and many lean into this reputation, earning a living doing perilous stunts at fairs and festivals. Some wranglers prefer to lead a quieter life, helping people better understand and commune with the beasts of **[[Everden]]**.

## Wrangler Training Tradition

When you choose this tradition at 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the **Animal Handling** skill if you don’t already have it. Your hand-to-hand combat skills mix with the talents of a herder.

- **Expert Herder:** When you make a Wisdom (Animal Handling) skill check, you add your **Dexterity modifier** to the check in addition to your Wisdom modifier and proficiency bonus.
    
- **Whip Mastery:** You gain proficiency with the **whip**, and it is considered a **monk weapon** for you.
    
- **Beast Bond:** By spending 10 minutes communing with a beast that is friendly to you, you can regain **ki points equal to your Wisdom modifier**. You must finish a long rest before you can use this feature again.

## Monster Rider

At 3rd level, you gain the ability to hop across the battlefield on the backs of larger creatures, as well as maneuver creatures using your whip.

- **Lasso:** When you make a successful attack roll with your whip against a creature that is Medium or Large, you can forgo doing damage to **grapple** the creature instead. The **DC to escape = 8 + your proficiency bonus + your Dexterity modifier**. The grapple ends if you are disarmed or drop the whip.
    
- **Mount:** When you have a nonhumanoid creature larger than you grappled by your whip, you can use a bonus action to spend **1 ki point** and immediately pull yourself up onto its back or shoulders. This ends the grapple. While mounted on a beast, monstrosity, or plant, you have **advantage on Animal Handling checks** made to guide it.
    

---

## Monster Guider

At 6th level, your skill with your whip becomes more refined.

- **Extended Reach:** You can wield a whip with a reach 5 feet greater than normal (**15 feet**). This increases as you level: **20 feet** at 10th level, **25 feet** at 14th level, and **30 feet** at 17th level.
    
- **Versatile Lasso:** You can now lasso creatures that are Small, Medium, Large, or Huge.
    
- **Maneuver:** When you successfully grapple a creature with your whip, you can spend **1 ki point** to move that creature a number of feet up to the reach of your whip in any direction.
    
- **Ki Commune:** As an action, you can spend **1 ki point** to cast the _Speak with Animals_ spell without components.
    

---

## Leader of the Herd

At 11th level, you give off an aura that endears you to animals.

- **Charm Beast:** As an action, you can spend **2 ki points** to target a beast within 30 feet. The target is **charmed** by you for 10 minutes and understands rudimentary commands. You can have a number of creatures charmed equal to your **Wisdom modifier**.
    
- **Spectral Stampede:** As an action, you can spend **6 ki points** to cast the _[[HW2 Spells#Spectral Stampede|Spectral Stampede]]_ spell without components. Your spellcasting ability is Wisdom.
    

---

## Freedom of the Plains

At 17th level, almost nothing can slow you down. You **can’t be restrained**, and you **ignore difficult terrain**.

## Lasso Master

At 17th level, your skill with your whip is unparalleled.

- **Any Size:** You can use your whip to lasso creatures of any size. To use your Wrangler features on a **Gargantuan** creature, you must succeed on a contested check (Your Wisdom (Animal Handling) vs. Target's Strength (Athletics)).
    
- **Whip Counter:** When a creature within reach hits you with an attack, you can use your reaction and spend **3 ki points** to make a whip attack against them. On a hit, you deal no damage but **grapple** the creature and **reduce the damage you take to 0**. You must declare this before damage is rolled.
