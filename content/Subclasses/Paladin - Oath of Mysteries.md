# Oath of Mysteries Paladin

The Oath of Mysteries commands paladins to seek out harmful secrets and carefully shepherd the disclosure of dangerous truths. Lost histories and new frontiers call to these paladins who seek to understand the unsolved enigmas of the world. They relentlessly carry out investigations by studying clues, conspiracies, and conundrums from multiple angles. Though they may present themselves as gentle scholars, these paladins are capable warriors due to their keen ability to use magic and spot an opponent's weaknesses.

---

## Tenets of Mysteries

All paladins of the Oath of Mysteries share some variation of the first two tenets; however, the latter two represent an ideological split regarding the sharing of information.

- **Seek Truth.** Cut away the deceptions that cloud your mind with the sharp blade of truth.
    
- **Knowledge is Power.** To ensure victory, learn more of your enemy than they learn of you.
    
- **Wield Wisdom Wisely.** For the safety of all, there must be a time to speak honestly and a time to withhold.
    
- **Spread Understanding.** Secrets are poison to true harmony. Share what you have learned.

## Channel Divinity

When you take this oath at 3rd level, you gain the following two Channel Divinity options.

### Mystery of Concealment

As an action, you can hide yourself and others from magical detection. Choose a number of creatures up to your proficiency bonus within 20 feet of you. For the next hour, you and these creatures can’t be affected by any divination magic or perceived through magical scrying sensors. If you or any of these creatures would be targeted in this manner, you become aware of the divination spell and as a reaction, you can choose to disrupt it with an illusion. Additionally, all targets gain a bonus to **Stealth** checks equal to your **Charisma modifier** (minimum of +1) while they are within 20 feet of you.

### Mystery of Revelation

As a bonus action, you examine a creature within 30 feet of you that you can see. You become aware of any **damage resistances or vulnerabilities** the target has, and for 1 minute, whenever you hit the target with an attack, you deal an additional **1d8 damage** of a type of your choosing.

## Investigator Training

At 3rd level, you gain proficiency in the **History** and **Investigation** skills. Whenever you make an ability check using the History or Investigation skill, you gain a bonus to the check equal to your **Charisma modifier** (minimum of +1).

## Aura of Inquiry

Starting at 7th level, you emit an observant aura while you aren’t incapacitated. You and friendly creatures within **10 feet** of you can make Wisdom (**Perception**) checks as a bonus action and add a bonus to Perception and Insight checks equal to your **proficiency bonus**.

When you reach 18th level in this class, the range of this aura increases to **30 feet**.

## Chastise the Dishonest

At 15th level, you develop the ability to spot deception and punish liars. As an action, you can extend an aura of truth around yourself in a 20-foot radius that lasts for 10 minutes. When a creature in the affected area tells a deliberate lie, you know it is lying and can choose to use your reaction to force the creature to make a Charisma saving throw. A creature takes **radiant damage equal to 2d10 + your Charisma modifier** on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful save.

## Achieve Illumination

At 20th level, the mysteries of the world unfold before you. As an action, you can magically become infused with unyielding truth, gaining the following benefits for 1 minute:

- You have **truesight** out to a range of 120 feet.
    
- You can understand all spoken, signed, and written languages.
    
- On your turn, you can use a bonus action to cause a creature within 60 feet of you that you can see to become **vulnerable to a damage type of your choice** until the end of your next turn.
    

You can’t use this feature again until you finish a long rest.
