# Deep-Roots Sorcerer

The world of **[[Everden]]** hums with natural magic. Some folk possess the innate ability to absorb, store, and redirect this energy within themselves. Deep-roots sorcerers may have been born with this skill or showed an aptitude for it from an early age. Others have become attuned to the resonance of the world through years of patient and intentional contemplation.

These sorcerers are often marked by their connection to the landscape, taking on physical features that mirror the terrain around them. As living batteries, they can absorb massive amounts of arcane power not only from nature but also from other magic users.

## Sorcerous Mark

As you absorb energy from your surroundings, you become physically and emotionally changed. Natural magic leaves its mark on your body, a sign of your close connection to the environment. Choose a sorcerous mark from the table below or roll to determine it randomly.

### Deep-Roots Sorcerous Marks

|**d6**|**Mark**|
|---|---|
|1|One of your features, such as your horns, spines, or talons, is composed of local stone.|
|2|Your eyes always reflect the place where you were born rather than where you are now.|
|3|Regional plants sprout from your head.|
|4|When you move from one terrain to another, your personality shifts.|
|5|When injured, you bleed sap rather than blood.|
|6|Small animals tend to be relaxed around you and may nest in your fur, feathers, or clothes.|

## Grounded Magic

Starting at 1st level, you learn new deep-roots sorcerer spells when you reach the sorcerer levels listed on the Deep-Roots Spells table. Deep-roots spells count as sorcerer spells, but don’t count against the number of sorcerer spells you know.

### Deep-Roots Spells

| **Sorcerer Level** | **Spells**                            |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------- |
| 1st                | _Create or Destroy Water_, _Entangle_ |
| 3rd                | _Barkskin_, _Shatter_                 |
| 5th                | _Revivify_, _Vampiric Touch_          |
| 7th                | _Control Water_, _Stone Shape_        |
| 9th                | _Commune with Nature_, _Hallow_       |

## Natural Battery

Starting from 1st level, you store arcane energy within your body. Your Natural Battery dice pool represents how much arcane energy you currently have stored. When you are hit by an attack that deals magical damage, or cold, fire, force, lightning, or radiant damage, you can use your reaction to halve the damage you take and add **1d6** to your Natural Battery dice pool. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

When you damage a creature with a spell, you can spend any number of dice from your Natural Battery dice pool to add a bonus to the spell’s damage. You choose the type of bonus damage: **cold, fire, force, or lightning damage**. You lose any remaining dice in your Natural Battery dice pool when you finish a long rest.

When you reach 9th level in this class, your Natural Battery dice increase to **d8s**, and when you reach 17th level, they increase to **d10s**.

## In Tune with the Land

At 1st level, you know the **Druidcraft** cantrip and can understand and speak **[[Terran]]**, the elemental language of earth.

## Land’s Harmony

At 6th level, your connection with the land deepens and further alters you. Depending on the terrain in which you most recently finished a long rest, you gain one benefit from the Land’s Harmony Effects table. The effect lasts until you complete your next long rest.

### Land’s Harmony Effects

| **Terrain** | **Benefit**                                                                                             |
| ----------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Arctic      | You gain resistance to **cold damage**.                                                                 |
| Coast       | You can cast the _Water Breathing_ spell once per day without expending a spell slot.                   |
| Desert      | You gain resistance to **fire damage**.                                                                 |
| Forest      | You can cast the _Speak with Plants_ spell once per day without expending a spell slot.                 |
| Grassland   | Your walking speed increases by **5 feet**, and you can use the **Disengage** action as a bonus action. |
| Mountain    | You can cast the _Call Lightning_ spell once per day without expending a spell slot.                    |
| Swamp       | You ignore nonmagical **difficult terrain**.                                                            |
| Underground | You gain **darkvision** out to 60 feet (or extend existing darkvision by 30 feet).                      |

---

## Taking Root

Starting at 14th level, your connection with nature further energizes you. When you take a short rest, you can add one die to your Natural Battery dice pool. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your Charisma modifier, and you regain all expended uses when you finish a long rest.

## Energy Transformation

At 18th level, your control over the power that gathers within you becomes more disciplined. As a bonus action, you can convert any number of dice in your Natural Battery dice pool into sorcery points and vice versa.
