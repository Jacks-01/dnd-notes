# Circle of the Warden

Every druid has a responsibility to the natural world, but those who join the Circle of the Warden stand as sentinels of nature’s balance. These druids specialize in divination and abjuration magic to help foresee possible imbalances and correct them before they cause irreparable damage. To the druids of this circle, people are as much a part of the natural balance as plants and animals.

In **[[Humblewood]]**, many druids who have joined the **[[Tenders]]** of the **[[Scorched Grove]]** are members of this circle, as its values align quite closely with the tenets of their order.

---

## Heartbeat of the Land

Starting when you choose this circle at 2nd level, you form a connection to the natural world around you.

- **Natural Insight:** You gain **expertise** in the **Nature** and **Insight** skills. Your proficiency bonus is doubled for any ability check you make that uses either of those proficiencies.
    
- **Commune with Nature:** By spending 10 minutes communing with nature, you can glean the following information about the area within **3 miles** of you:
    
    - The general location and severity of any immediate environmental threats (forest fires, flooding, blights, etc.).
        
    - The presence of any aberrations, beasts, elementals, fey, or undead (but not their specific locations).
        
    - The general direction of the nearest portal to an elemental plane.
        

---

## Reclamation

Also at 2nd level, you learn to siphon off and shoulder harmful elemental energy to protect your allies.

- **Siphon Damage:** When a creature within 30 feet of you takes **cold, fire, lightning, or thunder damage**, you can use your reaction to grant that creature **resistance** against the damage dealt. You take damage equal to the amount taken by the target creature.
    
- **Clear Hazard:** As an action, you can reduce the area of natural or magical effects that deal persistent cold, fire, or lightning damage within 30 feet of you. You may remove up to a **20-foot cube** from the area.
    
    - For each 5-foot space removed, you take **1d4 damage** of the same type.
        
    - If the effect is a spell, you take **1d4 damage per the spell’s level** for each 5-foot space removed. If no damage-dealing spaces remain, the spell is dispelled.
        

---

## Sympathetic Shield

Starting at 6th level, you can channel your Wild Shape energy into a protective spirit. As an action, you can expend a use of **Wild Shape** to shield yourself or an ally within 30 feet with translucent spirit armor.

- **Protection:** The shielded creature gains **1d10 temporary hit points** for every 3 druid levels you possess and a **+1 bonus to AC**. The shield lasts until the temporary hit points are expended or replaced.
    
- **Retaliation (14th Level):** Whenever a shielded creature is dealt damage by a target within 5 feet, the spirit armor deals **1d8 magical bludgeoning, piercing, or slashing damage** (chosen when the shield is activated) to that target. It can hit multiple targets per round, but only once per target.
    

---

## Aura of Calm

Starting at 10th level, your presence radiates soothing natural magic in a **10-foot aura**.

- **Enhanced Healing:** Whenever you or a friendly creature within the aura receives healing from a spell, the spell restores additional hit points equal to **half your druid level**.
    
- **Vibrant Growth:** Non-creature plants within the aura grow thick and restored to full vigor. These plants become safe for travelers, negating difficult terrain (including from spells like _Entangle_ or _Spike Growth_) and granting **advantage on saving throws** against the harmful effects of such spells.
    

---

## Bond of Shelter

Once you reach 14th level, nature itself rises to protect you. As an action, you can create a **30-foot radius, 20-foot tall cylinder** of protective energy centered on you. It lasts for 1 hour and remains stationary.

Natural features (plants, rocks, water, or elemental forces) animate to defend the area. You choose any number of creatures to be immune to the effects; all others are subject to the following:

- **Warded Entry:** Creatures cannot willingly enter the cylinder unless they succeed on a **Charisma saving throw** against your spell save DC. The area is also protected against planar travel (Astral, Ethereal, Feywild, Shadowfell, or _Plane Shift_).
    
- **Defensive Buffer:** Creatures have **disadvantage on attack rolls** against targets within the cylinder.
    
- **Mental Fortitude:** Targets of your choice within the cylinder cannot be **charmed, frightened, or possessed**.
    
- **Animated Strike:** When a creature enters the cylinder for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there, it takes **5d10 magical bludgeoning damage**.
    

Once you use this feature, you can’t do so again until you finish a long rest.
