# Warlock Patron: The Whispering Wind

The wind itself has spoken to you, and now wants to speak through you. You may have been blessed by the Amaranthine **[[Sihlk]]**, or an ancient wind elemental too weak to speak for themselves. With these gifts, you can float messages to others a long distance, and use the air itself to show powerful visions that encourage or terrify others.

## Expanded Spell List

The Whispering Wind lets you choose from an expanded list of spells when you learn a warlock spell. The following spells are added to the warlock spell list for you.

### Expanded Spells

| **Spell Level** | **Spells**                       |
| --------------- | -------------------------------- |
| 1st             | _Entangle_, _Feather Fall_       |
| 2nd             | _Knock_, _Levitate_              |
| 3rd             | _Clairvoyance_, _Sending_        |
| 4th             | _Confusion_, _Phantasmal Killer_ |
| 5th             | _Cone of Cold_, _Wall of Force_  |

## Voice of the Wind

Starting at 1st level, you learn to send the wind's whispers far and wide.

- **Whispered Reach:** You know the **Message** cantrip; you can cast it as a bonus action, and its range is increased to **1,000 feet**. As long as you know the general location of the target and they are within range, they receive the message.
    
- **Shaped Air:** You know the **Silent Image** spell and always have it prepared. You can cast it without expending a spell slot a number of times equal to your **Charisma modifier**, regaining all uses after a long rest. Whenever you cast it, you can choose one of the following additional effects:
    
    - **A Vision of Courage:** Up to 4 other creatures of your choice who can see the illusion gain **2d4 temporary hit points**.
        
    - **A Vision of Terror:** Up to 4 creatures of your choice who can see the illusion must make a Wisdom save against your spell save DC or be **frightened** of you for as long as you maintain concentration.

---

## Nature’s Force

Starting at 6th level, you are imbued with safety from the wind and can add its power to your magic.

- **Cold Resistance:** You have resistance to **cold damage**.
    
- **Chilling Gale:** You can substitute the damage type of any spell you cast to be **cold damage**. You can do this a number of times equal to your proficiency bonus, regaining all uses after a long rest.
    
- **Buffeting Blast:** When a spell you cast deals cold damage to a target, they must make a Constitution saving throw against your spell save DC or be **knocked back 10 feet**.

---

## Master Storyteller

Beginning at 10th level, your ability to tell stories with the wind reaches new heights. When you cast _silent image_, the additional effect you choose can affect **any number of creatures**. Additionally, you gain two new options for the spell:

- **A Vision of Flight:** Any number of creatures of your choice who can see the illusion gain a **fly speed** equal to their walking speed for the duration. This ends immediately if concentration is lost.
    
- **A Vision of Warriors:** Any number of creatures of your choice who can see the illusion must make a Dexterity saving throw, taking **4d8 force damage** on a failure, or half as much on a success.

---

## Word of the Wind

Starting at 14th level, your ability to speak the word of the wind is unparalleled. When you cast _silent image_, you can choose **two** additional effects simultaneously.

Additionally, when you cast the _message_ cantrip, you can choose one of the following enhanced effects:

- **Mass Message:** Target any number of people within **1 mile**, but the message is limited to five words and they cannot reply.
    
- **Planar Whisper:** Target someone on a **different plane**, though the target learns which plane you are currently on.
    
- **Projected Casting:** Use _message_ to target someone within range with a spell that has a **verbal component**. You must still use your action and a spell slot to cast the second spell. Once used, you can’t use this effect again until you finish a long rest.
