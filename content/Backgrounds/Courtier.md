# Courtier (Background)

You grew up in the court, watching the fortunes of nobles rise and fall through complex plots and subtle schemes. Trained in courtly manners, you can navigate complex social situations with grace. You understand that a well-placed word can be just as devastating as a dagger in the back. Though you have enjoyed a measure of power and influence, you know that you are just a small link in a long chain of alliances and enmities and that folk far more powerful than you could end your career in an instant. Early on, you learned that trust is just another resource to be gathered and spent.

Now, something has taken you away from the familiar social dangers of the court. Were you sent away in disgrace, or did you flee to find a different kind of life for yourself? Perhaps you are still working for the court clandestinely or in the open? No matter what, the court has left its talon marks on your soul.

**Skill Proficiencies:** Insight and your choice of either Deception or Persuasion

**Tool Proficiencies:** Forgery Kit

**Languages:** One of your choice

**Equipment:** An exceptionally fine dagger, a letter from a family member or mentor, an antique piece of jewelry, a set of fine clothes, and a purse containing **25 gp**.

---

## Feature: Courtly Manners

Your masterful grasp of etiquette makes you welcome in the highest social circles and courts wherever you go. However, you understand that nothing comes for free in these environments. Nobles and other court figures may ask you to give something to them in exchange for access to the court, such as information or a subtle bribe.
