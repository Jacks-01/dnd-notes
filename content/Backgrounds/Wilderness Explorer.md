# Wilderness Explorer (Background)

Due to your experience navigating the world's wild places, you feel most at home away from the hustle and bustle of perches and cities. You can get your bearings almost anywhere using nature as your guide.

**Skill Proficiencies:** **Nature**, **Survival** **Tool Proficiencies:** **Herbalism Kit** **Languages:** One of your choice

**Equipment:** A hatchet, a journal, a hooded lantern, a set of traveler’s clothes, and a pouch of 20 smooth pebbles.

---

## Feature: Survivor

You are accustomed to traveling the wilderness and keeping those you guide safe from danger. By examining your surroundings, you can discern signs of danger, such as markings left by local wild creatures, natural hazards (like quicksand or toxic plants), and terrain hazards (like pitfalls or cliffs).

The GM decides what information you have access to and may require an Intelligence (**Nature**) or Wisdom (**Survival**) check for specific details. Additionally, if you ever become lost in a familiar natural environment, you can find your bearings by spotting key landmarks or constellations in the night sky.
