# Stonesinger (Background)

You are a practitioner of stonesinging, an ancient art of the **[[Hedge|hedges]]**. This musical tradition has been passed down through generations, combining chants and percussion with the natural resonant tones of rocks. Stonesinging is often a communal art, with choirs of stonesingers all across **[[Everden]]**, each with their own traditions but all sharing the central tenet of respect for stones.

It is said that, long ago, the stones responded to skilled stonesingers with music of their own. The question of why the stones have fallen silent haunts stonesingers of today—anyone who finds a way to reawaken them will be hailed as the greatest of all stonesingers.

**Skill Proficiencies:** **Insight**, **Performance** **Tool Proficiencies:** One type of **musical instrument** **Languages:** **[[Terran]]** **Equipment:** A musical instrument (one of your choice), a rock hammer, a songbook, a set of traditional robes, and a pouch containing **15 gp**.

---

## Feature: Stonewise

You are attuned to the sounds of the earth. You can easily identify rocks and gems by tapping on them as an action to learn their properties and value in gp. You have advantage on Wisdom (**Perception**) checks while traveling through rocky terrain, and you can easily spot landslides, crevasses, and other natural hazards involving rocks without needing to make a skill check.
