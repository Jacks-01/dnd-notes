# Wonderstruck (Background)

At some point in your life, you felt the brush of an Amaranthine’s wing or paw. The experience changed you forever as they gave you a glimpse into the immensity of the **[[Great Rhythm]]** and your place within it. Whether it was a moment of profound crisis or sudden clarity, the divine resonance of the encounter still echoes in your soul.

**Skill Proficiencies:** **Investigation**, **Religion** **Tool Proficiencies:** One type of **artisan’s tools** or **musical instrument** **Languages:** One of your choice

**Equipment:** A journal, an amulet bearing the symbol of an Amaranthine, a map of local shrines, a set of common clothes, and a pouch containing **15 gp**.

---

## Amaranthine Experiences

You can select what you experienced from the table below or roll randomly to determine which of the **[[Humblewood Campaign/Amaranthines/Overview]]** touched your life.

|**d10**|**Experience**|
|---|---|
|1|As you gazed at a red sunrise, you saw **[[Khaluz]]** looking back at you. Her stare burned into you.|
|2|In a moment of weakness, you felt the stomp of **[[Arghan]]**’s mighty hoof urging you to find strength.|
|3|As you lay broken, a silent vision of **[[Ostelo]]** appeared before you, and your pain vanished.|
|4|You had a near-death experience, but **[[Tyton]]** the Nightfather ushered you back to the living.|
|5|In a moment of high emotion, you heard the rhythmic song of **[[Gebbo]]**’s eternal revel.|
|6|You dreamt of **[[Gesme]]**, Amaranthine of Invention, wreathed in flame; you awoke with buzzing ideas.|
|7|Alone and scared, you heard the whispers of **[[Hath]]** and saw a path of stars guiding you home.|
|8|When you swore an oath, you felt the claws of **[[Ranzang]]** on your shoulders; he is watching.|
|9|You met a vulpin with a hungry smile who advised you on your desires; she said her name was **[[Kren]]**.|
|10|Walking in the dark, the invisible presence of **[[Eresutha]]** led you to uncover something long hidden.|

---

## Feature: Reverberations

After encountering an Amaranthine, you can still hear divine resonances that grow stronger the less attached you are to this world. Whenever you make a **death saving throw**, you experience another vision from the Amaranthine that gives you some new insight—should you survive. The GM determines the nature of this vision.
