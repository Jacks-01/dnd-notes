# Warrenborn (Background)

You grew up in the winding tunnels of one of **[[Wesden]]**’s mountain warrens, embraced by a tight-knit community. Your neighbors were as close as family—dozens of aunts, uncles, and cousins. Beyond the warren door, you were taught how to survive in the difficult landscape, but you always knew a warm welcome awaited at home. While your warren was cozy, it could also be stifling for someone who longed for adventure.

**Skill Proficiencies:** **Persuasion**, **Survival** **Tool Proficiencies:** One type of **artisan’s tools** and one type of **musical instrument** **Equipment:** A farewell letter from a friend, a necklace bearing the sign of your warren, an **Herbalism Kit**, a set of common clothes, and a pouch containing **15 gp**.

---

## Feature: Warren Home

From your earliest days in the tight quarters of the warren, you learned the importance of getting along with others. Outside of combat, you are skilled at calming inflamed tempers and helping people understand each other’s points of view.

You have advantage on Charisma (**Persuasion**) checks made to interact with non-hostile humanoids. You also have ties to a specific warren and can always find a warm welcome, healing, supplies, and shelter when you visit. Other warrens will likely extend the same hospitality to you.
