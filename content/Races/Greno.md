Greno are a folk similar to frogs and toads. They live comfortably in water and on land thanks to their amphibious nature and webbed hands and feet that make them excellent swimmers.

![[content/Media/1 2.webp]]

## **Of Land and Lake**

The famed variation and adaptability of the Greno are rooted in their origin tale. Legend tells of an island at the center of a vast swamp, where the first greno spawning pool was created. Isolated from the greater world for a long time, the greno who lived on the island were fairly uniform—amphibious, sturdy, but all very similar to each other. Over time, the needs of the island grew to a point where the greno could not survive without stepping out into the lands beyond the swamp. Some took to the trees, becoming the veret greno, others ventured into dangerous lands, developing unique defenses and becoming the venim greno. Finally, the ones who remained behind to defend the first spawning pool became the resilient and sturdy verru greno.

Today, greno of all kinds mingle together regularly—but they always honor their differences and the unique talents they each contribute to greno society.

## **Spawning Pools**

Young grenos have a unique childhood compared to many other folk in Everden. Greno parents lay soft eggs in safe pools of water. When these hatch into greno tadpoles, the infants stay in the pool for their first few years of life until they develop the ability to breathe air. Tadpoles are tended to and taught by many adults rather than their specific parents. Greno adolescents emerge from their childhood pools ready to explore the world. As infant grenos are very vulnerable, the location of their pools is a closely held secret in some places. Meanwhile, in some larger cities and towns, greno spawning pools are public, though still vigilantly guarded.

# **Healing and Folk Magic**

There are two strong arcane traditions practiced among grenos. First is a focus on healing magic, which is pursued diligently by many grenos. Healers of any tradition are respected in greno culture for their expertise and dedication to helping others. Second and more widespread is a deep folk magic that some would describe as superstitions. Greno folk magic doesn’t have the flashy effects of other arcane practices. Instead, it holds that practicing minor rituals, like carrying a lucky pebble, blinking three times whenever you see the moon during the daytime, or humming while stirring soup clockwise, can bring prosperity and good luck. Some greno mages spend their entire careers trying to prove or disprove the effectiveness of this folk magic. Despite a lack of conclusion one way or another, many grenos still chose to honor the old ways.

# **Greno Names**

As greno aren’t raised by their parents, they get their names from their siblings or name themselves. Therefore, greno names are highly varied. They are often drawn from nature or the cultures that young grenos grew up around. Grenos use the name of their spawning pool as their family name.

**Greno names:** Adena, Chive, Firebelly, Ghost, Hoppert, Leopel, Lollyhops, Marshall, Pelob, Pipid  
**Spawning Pool Names:** Deepery, Etangblanc, Havre, Hollowell, Leaflock, Longrain, Middlepuddle, Quickmarsh, Sautsaut, Spawnhaven

# **GRENO TRAITS**

### **Ability Score Increases.**

Increase three ability scores of your choice by 1, or increase one ability score by 2 and another ability score by 1. To play a typical venim greno choose Constitution and Wisdom. To play a typical veret greno choose Wisdom and Dexterity. To play a typical verru greno choose Wisdom and Strength.

### **Alignment.**

Once they emerge from their spawning pools, Greno often embrace their new freedom and tend towards chaotic alignments.

### **Speed.**

Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You have a swimming speed equal to your walking speed.

### **Age.**

Gernos emerge from their tadpole stage around the age of 12, at which point they are considered adults. They have average life spans, with many grenos naturally reaching the age of 75.

### **Size.**

Gernos tend towards either streamlined builds or round ones. Your size is Medium or Small.

### **Amphibious.**

You can breathe air and water.

### **Bombastic Croak.**

As a bonus action, when you make a weapon attack or a spell attack that has a verbal component, you can empower your attack with a war croak. If your attack hits, it is considered a critical hit. Your croak can be heard 1,000 feet away. Once you have used this ability, you can’t use it again until you finish a long rest.

### **Jumper.**

Your long jump is a distance equal to your walking speed and your high jump is a distance equal to half your walking speed, with or without a running start.

### **Languages.**

You can speak, read, and write Birdfolk and Greno. The Greno language uses different grammar when spoken underwater or in the air.

### **Lineage Options.**

You can choose the venim, veret, or verru lineage option.

## **Venim**

Inspired by poisonous frogs, these flamboyant grenos warn of their dangerous skin with vibrant colors.

### **Poison Skin (3/Day).**

When a creature touches you or hits you with a melee weapon attack, you can use your reaction to force that creature to make a Constitution saving throw (DC equals 8 + your Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus). On a failed save, the creature takes poison damage equal to double your proficiency bonus and is poisoned until the end of your next turn.

### **Warning Colors.**

You are proficient in the Intimidation skill.

---

## **Veret**

Inspired by wood frogs, grass frogs, and green frogs, these adaptable grenos are particularly sensitive to their environments.

### **Sticky Feet.**

You have a climbing speed equal to your walking speed.

### **Sensitive Skin.**

You can cast the detect poison and disease spell at will without the need for verbal or material components. When you reach 5th level, you can also cast the protection from poison spell once a day without expending a spell slot. Wisdom is your spellcasting ability for these spells.

---

## **Verru**

Inspired by toads, these tough grenos are well-adapted to life on land and have a reputation for great endurance.

### **Unslowed Toad.**

You have advantage on saving throws to resist gaining levels of exhaustion and being petrified.

### **Warty Skin.**

You have resistance to slashing damage.
