# Seeta

Seetas are a diverse group of parrot- and toucan-like birdfolk with colorful plumage and prominent beaks. Preferring warm weather, many seetas make their home in treetop communities throughout the **[[Tanglewilds]]**.

![[seeta.webp]]

## Lifelong Learners

Seetas enjoy a longer childhood than most birdfolk and are not considered adults until they reach their third decade. This extended adolescence is usually spent studying various disciplines, and seeta parents watch over their chicks’ early education with great enthusiasm. Though books are part of a seeta’s education, talons-on experience is considered far more valuable. 

Once young seetas have fledged, they often embark on journeys far from home to learn from different folks and communities. In adulthood, this passion for learning does not dim. An interest in novelty and a flexible way of thinking is as much a part of an elderly seeta’s life as one who has just hatched.

## Beaks and Brains

Seeta have strong beaks capable of doing significant harm. For this reason, seetas can often find work as hired muscle, even if they aren’t trained for combat. Their dramatic plumage and large beaks are intimidating enough that they rarely have to resort to violence. Indeed, many seetas tend to be gentle and thoughtful, preferring to cultivate their intellect rather than their fighting skills. Those seetas who do choose to take up arms are often drawn to the study of tactics.

Typically, seetas have an easy time making friends with folks who others perceive as odd or eccentric. They are gregarious but can become easily bored. This, combined with a knack for using tools in unconventional ways, means that if a seeta is not sufficiently entertained, they will make their own fun, especially if that fun involves taking something apart.

## Expert Imitators

Another talent possessed by seetas is their uncanny knack for remembering sounds, coupled with an uncanny ability to mimic them. Seetas are impressed by those who have accumulated a mental library of many sounds, and such individuals are praised in seeta culture for their worldliness. While some seetas employ their talent for subterfuge and trickery, others use it for noble ends, such as carrying short but important messages. Using their keen ear for sounds, many seetas become expert linguists and translators.

## Seeta Names

Traditional seeta names grow with an individual as they age. A seeta chick is given a single syllable nestname by their caretakers. Throughout their life, seetas add to this name whenever they feel inspired. Some seeta names exceed several dozen syllables in their old age, and a long name is a sign of a life well lived.

**Nestnames:** Ai, Bee, Bell, Ben, Cha, El, Eve, Gru, Ha, Kin, Ko, Lea, Moo, Nite, Nyo, One, Peak, Ree, Tha

**Family Names:** BlueEl, EnMi, GyoVa, MooTha, MossLit, NiteOn, NyoCha, ReeWish, TsuTweh

## Seeta Traits

The seeta species is inspired by parrots, cockatiels, toucans, and other tropical birds.

### Ability Score Increase
Increase one ability score by 2 and another ability score by 1. To play a typical seeta, choose Intelligence and Strength. Alternatively, increase three ability scores by 1.

### Creature Type
You are a humanoid.

### Age
Seeta fledge into their adult plumage around the age of 15, but they are not considered adults until the age of 30. They are among the longest-living folk in **[[Everden]]**, and it’s not uncommon for a seeta to reach the age of 150.

### Size
Seetas have a wide variety of builds and have heights ranging from 3 to 6 feet tall. Your size is Medium or Small.

### Speed
Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

### Glide
Using your feathered arms, you can slow your fall and glide short distances. When falling, you can use your reaction to spread your arms, stiffen your wing feathers, and slow your descent. While doing so, you continue to fall gently at a speed of 60 feet per round, taking no fall damage when you land. 

If you would fall at least 10 feet in this way, you may fly up to your movement speed in any direction you choose, except upward, and land in the space you finish your movement. You cannot glide while carrying heavy weapons or wielding a shield (though you may drop any held items as part of your reaction to spread your arms). You cannot glide while wearing heavy armor or if you are encumbered.

### Wing Flap
As a bonus action, you can use your powerful feathered arms to propel yourself upward a distance equal to half your speed. You can use it in conjunction with a regular jump, but not while gliding.

### Strong Beak
Your large, curved beak is a natural weapon that you can use to make unarmed strikes. On a hit, you deal **piercing damage equal to 1d6 + your Strength modifier** instead of the bludgeoning damage normal for an unarmed strike.

### Striking Plumage
You have proficiency in the **Intimidation** skill.

### The Knack
You can turn your talons to many crafts. You have proficiency with **two sets of artisan’s tools** of your choice. You can also use these tools in unconventional ways; you can use artisan’s tools that you are proficient with to attempt a check that would usually require a different set of tools. When using tools in this way, you add **half your proficiency bonus** (rounded down) to the check.

### Mimicry
You have the innate ability to convincingly replicate any sound you’ve heard, such as a voice, a spell being cast, or the sound of a weapon striking stone. A creature that hears the sounds can tell they are imitations with a successful Wisdom (**Insight**) check. The DC for this check equals **8 + your proficiency bonus + your Intelligence modifier** (minimum of 1).

### Languages
You can speak, read, and write **[[Birdfolk]]** and two other languages of your choice.
