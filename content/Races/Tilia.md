# Tilia

Tilias are reptilian folk known for their curiosity, openness, and joviality. Creativity and open-mindedness are cornerstones of tilia society, and those seen as sophisticated are held in great regard. The most respect, however, is afforded to those who can advance their agendas subtly through wit and charm.


![[tilia.webp]]

## Rhetoric and Revelry

Tilia culture celebrates an array of festivities believed to be necessary to keep the world turning. These revels generally center on important natural events, such as the solstices or the changing of seasons, and are overseen by the Amaranthine **[[Gebbo]]**, the tilias’ patron. The most superstitious tilias believe that refusing to participate in merrymaking angers Gebbo, who will bring misfortune to any spoilsports.

Tilias raised in this culture of revelry are taught to see life as a performance, an art form only perfected through a lifetime of different roles. For such tilias, true wisdom can only be gained through a plethora of experiences. Therefore, many tilia embark on journeys to learn more about the world, logging their travels both for entertainment and enlightenment.

## Curiosity and Creativity

Tilias enjoy seeking out the new and unfamiliar, devoted to broadening their perspectives and deepening their understanding of the world. By absorbing the different ways people draw meaning from the world, tilias can create a more vibrant picture of the world for one another. As a result, tilia culture is a vibrant patchwork of experiences, focused on learning, sharing, and self-expression.

## Playing Politics

Though tilias tend towards friendliness and cooperation, their interactions drip with intrigue. Those in positions of power must play a careful balancing act, subtly strengthening allies and weakening rivals without appearing rude or dull. For tilias, politics is a game that can be won or lost; some play fair, but others are more than willing to cheat.

---

## Experimental Temperaments

Tilias are renowned for their tradition of alchemy. Combining the knowledge of several different schools with the local flora of the **[[Tanglewilds]]**, they have made great strides in potioncraft, especially in the development of healing salves. Alchemists are highly respected within tilia culture, and the more experimental their brews, the higher their esteem.

## Scaled Tales

As the only common scaled folk on a continent filled with furred and feathered folk, tilias have many idiosyncrasies that can confuse others. Though tilias have a strong natural dislike for the cold, this does not stop them from exploring frosty locations such as the **[[Crystal Expanse]]**. Due to this, tilias have a reputation as peculiar and eccentric folk. They are often underestimated by others who don’t realize the depths of their plans and the hidden motives behind their strange behavior.

## Tilia Traits

The tilia species is inspired by geckos and other reptiles. They tend to prefer balmy climates and are found throughout the Tanglewilds and the windswept **[[Gasparian Isles]]**.

### Ability Score Increase
Increase one ability score by 2 and another ability score by 1. To play a typical tilia, choose Dexterity and Charisma. Alternatively, increase three ability scores by 1.

### Creature Type
You are a humanoid.

### Age
Tilia are considered adults by the time they are 15 and have an average life span between 50 and 60 years.

### Size
Tilias range from smaller builds that are just under 3 feet tall to around 5 feet tall. Your size is Medium or Small.

### Speed
Your base walking speed is 30 feet. You have a **climbing speed of 30 feet**.

### Darkvision
You can see in dim light within 60 feet of you as if it were bright light and in darkness as if it were dim light. You can’t discern color in darkness, only shades of gray.

### Forest Cunning
You have advantage on Dexterity (**Stealth**) checks made to hide in forests and jungles.

### Sticky Grip
Your hands and feet have special pads that allow you to climb in extraordinary ways. You can climb upside down or on difficult surfaces without making ability checks.

### Sticky Tongue
As a bonus action, you can grab an object with your tongue weighing **20 pounds or less** that you can see within 15 feet of you. Your tongue adheres to the object and pulls it toward you in a straight line. If the object is being held or carried, the creature can make an opposed Strength (Athletics) check against you. 

Alternatively, you can use your action to project your tongue at a surface within 20 feet, pulling you directly toward it.

### Purifying Skin
Your scales secrete an oil that protects you from infection. You have advantage on saving throws made to resist disease.

### Shedding Skin
When you take acid or poison damage, you can use your reaction to shed your skin, reducing the damage you take by half. You can also use this to end any ongoing damage effect caused by sticky material (e.g., alchemist’s fire). You can’t use this trait again until you complete a long rest.

### Scaly Subtlety
You have proficiency in either the **Deception** or **Persuasion** skill. Non-tilia humanoids have disadvantage on **Insight** checks made against you.

### Languages
You can speak, read, and write **[[Birdfolk]]** and **[[Tilian (Language)|Tilian]]**.
