> **BIRDFOLK AND WESFOLK**
>  Outside of the Humblewood region, the folk in the rest of Wesden don’t consider the difference between birds and non-birds to be a particularly important one. For example, in Humblewood, bird species are known as birdfolk and non-birdfolk are called humblefolk, but in Stormcrag, both would simply be Stormfolk. Beyond regional naming, the folk of Wesden are all referred to as Wesfolk.

## Variations
Species are simply a template for your race, within each species can be  all kinds of variation. For example Elurans could be more like housecats, or they could be tigers, or even jaguars. Get creative! Use the table below for even more examples of variations of different species look like across the world.
