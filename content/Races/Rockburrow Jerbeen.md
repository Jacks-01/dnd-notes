# Jerbeen, Rockburrow

Rockburrow jerbeens resemble chinchillas, pikas, degus, and other large and fluffy rodents. They are taller than their cousins, the fieldscurry jerbeens (originally called [[jerbeens]] in the **[[Humblewood]]** Campaign Setting). Rockburrows share a similar love of community with their smaller cousins and have a reputation as the most hospitable folk in **[[Wesden]]**.

![[rockburrow_jerbeen.webp]]

## Enduring and Intrepid

Originating from the arid and sparsely vegetated slopes of the mountains of **[[Stormcrag]]**, rockburrow jerbeens are tough and sturdy folk. Families of rockburrows have spread across Wesden, though they tend to prefer mountain air, making their homes at high elevations in the **[[Frostcaps]]** and **[[Horned Ridge]]** to the north and the **[[Oh’toh’oh Mountains]]** in the **[[Tanglewilds]]**.

Rockburrows can carry objects in their cheek pouches, storing them for later or hiding them from prying eyes. Their soft, dense fur protects them from whatever comes their way.

## Prudent Planners

Adapted to some of the toughest environments in Wesden, rockburrow jerbeen survive thanks to their careful preparation during times of plenty, allowing them to endure lean times. Traditional rockburrow settlements called **warrens** are cut into mountainsides and contain dozens of homes and storerooms where food gathered over the summer and fall is stored to be eaten during winter. Warren homes usually house three or four generations; some families have inhabited the same warren for hundreds of years.

In cities and communities where rockburrows live alongside other folk, they often build homes that replicate their traditional mountain warrens with no windows and extensive basements. When they are away from their homes, rockburrow jerbeens often carry large packs filled with every supply they might need for their journey—to be caught unprepared is a mortifying experience for a rockburrow.

## Heartfelt Hospitality

Rockburrows are more independent than their fieldscurry cousins, but they share a similar attitude to hospitality. Many rockburrow jerbeens live by the traditional saying, “The stranger I share my breakfast with today is the friend who shares their dinner with me tomorrow.” They share what they have with those in need with the understanding that next season they might be the ones requiring help.

## Rockburrow Names

Rockburrow names are made up of three parts: a given name, a family name, and a burrow name. Warren names identify the community a rockburrow calls home. When a rockburrow jerbeen joins a new community, they change their warren name to show their commitment to their new home.

**Given Names:** Almi, Cashan, Fifi, Inick, Jurau, Raju, Leeyo, Miski, Nina, Wayra

**Family Names:** Barrelkeeper, Dustdancer, Gatherbrush, Leafbluff, Longwhisker, Mistfur, Pebblepad, Screehopper, Sunsinger, Widetail

**Warren Names:** Cavernhearth, Flowering Peak, Frostguard, Greenspine Gate, Lonely Air, Many Rooms, Nightbolt, Shadowside, Six Doors, Yellowburrow

## Jerbeen Traits

**Rockburrow is a lineage option for the jerbeen species**. Rockburrows and fieldscurry jerbeen share the following traits:

### Ability Score Increase
Increase one ability score by 2 and another ability score by 1. To play a typical rockburrow jerbeen, choose Constitution and Wisdom. Alternatively, increase three ability scores by 1.

### Creature Type
You are a humanoid.

### Age
Jerbeens grow quickly, reaching maturity around 5 years. They are the shortest-lived of all the Wesden species, living about 40 years.

### Speed
Your base walking speed is 30 feet.

### Nimbleness
You can move through the space of any creature that is of a size larger than yours.

### Team Tactics
You can use the **Help** action as a bonus action.

### Languages
You can speak, read, and write **[[Birdfolk]]** and **[[Jerbeen (Language)|Jerbeen]]**.

### Lineage Options
You can choose the **rockburrow jerbeen** lineage option found below or the **fieldscurry jerbeen** lineage option found in the Humblewood Campaign Setting.

---

### Rockburrow Jerbeen
The rockburrow lineage is inspired by chinchillas but is a good fit for characters resembling pikas, hamsters, guinea pigs, and chipmunks.

**Size.** Rockburrow jerbeens have heavier and denser bodies than fieldscurry jerbeens and are slightly taller on average, standing between 2-and-a-half and 3-and-a-half feet tall. Your size is Small.

**Cheek Pouches.** Your cheeks have elastic pouches that can stretch to fit objects weighing up to **2 pounds**, no larger than **10 inches** on a side. While stored in this way, objects are concealed and have total cover. You can stow or retrieve an object in your cheek pouches as a bonus action.

**Fluff Up.** As a bonus action, you gain advantage on a Charisma (**Intimidation**) check against a Medium or Large creature before the end of your next turn.

**Thick Fur.** You have resistance to **nonmagical bludgeoning damage**.
